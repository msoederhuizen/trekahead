TrekAhead

Plan a multi-day hut-to-hut hike in minutes, from nearly 17,000 mountain huts, refuges, shelters and guesthouses across 11 countries.

What it does

  • Every bed in the mountains on one map, from an unmanned wilderness hut to a guesthouse with a hot shower. Tap one for the photographs, the amenities it offers, and the contact details to reserve your place
  • Build your trip by tapping the huts you want. Every day is measured as you go: distance, ascent, descent, hours on your feet, and the difficulty on the SAC scale from a valley stroll to a T6 scramble
  • Nineteen of Europe's great walks already laid out stage by stage, the Alta Via 1, the Tour du Mont Blanc and the Walker's Haute Route among them
  • Or let the app build a route around your own preferences: where you start, the distance you enjoy, the ascent you are happy with, the kind of places you want to sleep and the hardest trail you will take
  • Cable cars, gondolas and mountain trains, if you would rather not walk up
  • Add your own photographs, ratings and notes to a hut, so the next walker up the valley sets off better informed
  • Huts, hut pages and saved routes work with no signal, and the photos for the huts on your route are downloaded ahead of time. The map background and planning a new route both need a connection

Nearly 17,000 huts, refuges, shelters and mountain guesthouses across 52 regions in Switzerland, France, Italy, Austria, Germany, Slovenia, Spain, Portugal, Norway, Sweden and Finland.

No advertising, no tracking, and no account needed to look at anything. An email address and a password are asked for only when you save or share a route, send in a photo, or write a review. Your notes, your saved trips and the routes you have walked are then kept on your account, so they follow you to a new phone. Your location is used to draw your position on the map and stays on your phone unless you report a hut where you are standing. See the privacy policy.

Where the data comes from

Hut locations and details come from OpenStreetMap. Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons, refuges.info, Open Data Hub, the Club Alpino Italiano register, and the huts' and hotels' own websites. Every photograph is credited in the app to its source and, where stated, its photographer and licence.

Contact and support

Something wrong with a hut, a route that will not build, a photograph that should not be there, or an idea for what the app should do next? Write to trekaheadapp@gmail.com and you will get a reply.

A wrong or missing hut is quickest to fix from inside the app, where reporting one sends its position straight to the moderation queue.
